SM Black Star Promos

Cards in set 251
Release date
Release period November 2016 - November 2021

The SM Black Star Promos are a series of promotional cards tied in with the Sun & Moon Series of cards, which started with the release of the Alola Collection the same day as Pokémon Sun and Moon. With the introduction of this Series, the XY prefix for promotional cards was changed to SM.
