S-P Promotional cards

Cards in set 374*
Release date
Release period November 2019 - December 2022

The S-P Promotional cards are a series of Japanese promotional cards tied in with the Sword & Shield Era of the Pokémon Trading Card Game, which began on November 29, 2019 with the release of the V Starter Sets. All promotional cards released during this Era have the suffix S-P.
