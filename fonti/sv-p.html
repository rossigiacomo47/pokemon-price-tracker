SV-P Promotional cards

Cards in set 304*
Release date
Release period November 18, 2022 - January 2026

The SV-P Promotional cards are a series of Japanese promotional cards associated with the Scarlet & Violet Era of the Pokémon Trading Card Game, which began on November 18, 2022 with the release of 001/SV-P Pikachu as a Pokémon Scarlet and Violet early purchase bonus. All promotional cards released during this Era have the suffix SV-P and "PROMO" in place of an expansion code. Equivalent SV-P Promotional cards were also available in other Asian languages, including Traditional Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, and Korean.
