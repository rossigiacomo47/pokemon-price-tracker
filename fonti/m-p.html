M-P Promotional cards

Cards in set Ongoing
Release date
Release period August 1, 2025 - Present

The M-P Promotional cards are a series of Japanese promotional cards associated with the MEGA Series of the Pokémon Trading Card Game, which began on August 1, 2025 with the release of 001/M-P Chikorita in the first MEGA Series Pokémon Card Gym Promo Card Pack. All promotional cards released during this Series have the suffix M-P and "PROMO" in place of an expansion code. Equivalent M-P Promotional cards were also available in other Asian languages, including Traditional Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, and Korean.
