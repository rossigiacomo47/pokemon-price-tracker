SM-P Promotional cards

Cards in set 437*
Release date
Release period November 2016 - October 2019

The SM-P Promotional cards are a series of Japanese promotional cards released over the course of the Sun & Moon Era of the Pokémon Trading Card Game, which officially began on November 18, 2016 with the release of the Pikachu's New Friends collection. All promotional cards released during this Era have the suffix SM-P and feature a rectangular "PROMO" symbol instead of the previously-used black star symbol.
