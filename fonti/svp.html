SVP Black Star Promos

Cards in set 226
Release date
Release period January 2023 - October 2025

The SVP Black Star Promos are a series of promotional cards tied in with the Scarlet & Violet Series of cards, which started with the release of the Paldea Collection, nearly two months after the release of the Pokémon Scarlet and Violet games. With the introduction of this Series, the prefix used in the card numbering was removed, replaced with the addition of the SVP set code.
